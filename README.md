## Upcoming: New age rating content descriptors in App Store Connect  

###### November 02, 2026  
<p>Starting November 16, 2026, App Store Connect will include new content descriptors to help ensure your app’s age ratings accurately reflect its features and are in accordance with local regulations. </p><p>When completing the age rating questionnaire in App Store Connect, you’ll need to declare whether your app includes:</p><ul>
<li><strong>Shared Location:</strong> Sharing of real-world location with another person or group.</li>
<li><strong>Dating and Relationships:</strong> Features that enable dating or relationship connections between real people and/or a virtual companion that engages a user in sexual or romantic content. </li>
</ul><p>You must provide these declarations when submitting an app update or making changes to your age rating questionnaire. All apps must have these declarations completed by January 12, 2027.</p><p>Apps that include Dating and Relationships receive a minimum age rating of 18+. Apps that include Shared Location may not have an impact on their minimum age rating in some regions.</p><p>In Brazil, local regulations require an A16 rating for Shared Location and an A18 rating for Dating and Relationships. If you believe your app qualifies for a lower age rating in Brazil — for example, if your app provides location sharing specifically designed for parents and children — you can <a href="https://www.gov.br/mj/pt-br/assuntos/seus-direitos/classificacao-1">request an official rating</a> directly from Brazil’s <a href="https://www.gov.br/mj/pt-br/assuntos/seus-direitos/classificacao-1/paginas-classificacao-indicativa/guia-de-classificacao">Ministry of Justice and Public Security</a> (MJSP/Ministério da Justiça e Segurança Pública). Once verified, the official rating will display on the App Store in Brazil.</p><p><a href="https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions#brazil-age-rating-values">Learn more about Brazil age ratings</a> </p>
